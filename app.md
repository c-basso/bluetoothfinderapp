## Full App Store description

Find Bluetooth Device – Bluetooth scanner and proximity finder

Find Bluetooth Device is an iOS app for iPhone that helps you locate nearby lost Bluetooth gear. Use this app as a Bluetooth scanner and finder when you need to recover AirPods, earbuds, headphones, smartwatches, speakers, fitness trackers and other powered-on accessories in range.

If you misplace a device at home, at work, in your car or on public transport, Find Bluetooth Device scans nearby Bluetooth signals and shows a clear list with signal strength. Pick the device you want, then follow hot–cold proximity guidance and a percentage indicator so you walk toward it instead of searching blindly.

**What you can do:**

- Scan for nearby Bluetooth devices and see them in a clear list
- View signal strength for each device to understand how close it is
- Follow hot–cold style guidance that intensifies as you move closer
- Track proximity with a percentage indicator so you know when you are almost there
- See device details like name, manufacturer and model when available
- Use the app in homes, offices, gyms, cars, trains or other indoor spaces

**How it works:**

1. Open Find Bluetooth Device on your iPhone.
2. Start a scan to discover nearby Bluetooth devices.
3. Select the device you want to find from the list.
4. Move around while watching the proximity percentage and hot–cold indicator.
5. As the signal gets hotter and the percentage rises, you are getting closer.

**Who it's for:** AirPods and earbud owners, headphone users, smartwatch and fitness tracker users, anyone who wants a Bluetooth device finder without complex setup.

**Use cases:** find lost AirPods or a single earbud; locate wireless headphones in clutter; track down a smartwatch or fitness tracker; search for Bluetooth speakers and other small gadgets indoors.

# Keyword research (2026-07)

## Positioning insight

Almost every top-ranking article for "find lost AirPods" funnels users into Apple Find My and then tells them a third-party app can't help. That is only true for the Find My network itself — but a large share of searchers are locked out of Find My (never enabled it, secondhand AirPods, non-Apple earbuds, no iCloud access) or their device isn't a Find My device at all (Sony/Bose/JBL headphones, Galaxy Buds, speakers, fitness trackers). **Those searchers have no first-party solution and the highest download intent.** Our content honestly targets that gap: any powered-on Bluetooth device, no prior setup, hot–cold signal-strength guidance.

Existing search-interest data (`queries.csv`): "bluetooth finder app" = 100 (+30%), "bluetooth device finder" = 62 (+1%).

## Content rules (honesty = conversions + trust)

- The app finds **powered-on** Bluetooth devices **in range**. Never claim it finds dead/offline devices — instead explain what to do (dead-AirPods guide converts by teaching search strategy + "install now so next time it takes 2 minutes").
- AirPods case alone does not broadcast when closed with buds inside; the guide says so and explains what works.
- Compare with Find My factually — Find My is better for registered Apple devices that are far away; our app wins for nearby search, non-Apple gear, and no-setup situations.

## On-page SEO plan

- Landing: hero (H1 + subtitle + App Store CTA + icon), screenshots with keyword-rich alts, TL;DR summary block, how-it-works steps (HowTo schema), features, guides grid (internal links), comparison table (vs Find My vs generic scanner), FAQ (FAQPage schema, each answer links "Learn more" to its guide), final CTA, footer.
- Guides: question-as-H1, direct answer in first paragraph (featured-snippet format), step-by-step with the app (HowTo schema), mini-FAQ (FAQPage schema), related guides, App Store CTA. Article + BreadcrumbList schema.
